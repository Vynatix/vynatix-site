What we build

Vynatix builds production AI systems on foundation models, and says plainly what a model can and cannot do. That includes closed-domain assistants that answer only from approved documents, cite the source of every answer, and hand anything they cannot ground to a person.

We take a system from a rough first prototype to a tool your team uses every day, and we work in the open so you always know how it reaches its answers. Vynatix deploys these systems into your own environment, including on-prem and sovereign cloud.

  • Chatbots and assistants.

    Conversational assistants built on foundation models, for production use.

  • Retrieval and grounded answers.

    Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) that finds the right passages in your own documents and grounds each answer in them.

  • Agent and harness engineering.

    The scaffolding around a model that lets it take actions and be tested, built to run in production.

  • Workflow automation.

    Model-driven steps inside the workflows your team already runs.

  • MCP servers and integrations.

    Model Context Protocol servers that let an assistant read from, and act on, your own systems.

  • SDKs and developer tooling.

    Libraries and tools so your own engineers can build on what we deliver.

  • Evaluation, deployment and monitoring.

    Evaluation pipelines that tell you when the model is wrong, deployment into your environment including on-prem and sovereign cloud, and observability after it ships.

Questions

Common questions

The questions clients usually ask before a first call, answered the way we would answer them in the room.

What does Vynatix do?

Vynatix is a software consultancy in Gothenburg, Sweden, that does three things: application development, AI engineering and cloud platforms.

What sets the work apart is that our engineers sit inside your team, with no account layer between you and them, and you own the code we write.

  • Application development, the largest share: web and native mobile, Android Automotive, wearables and VR.
  • AI engineering: production systems built on foundation models.
  • Cloud platforms: built on GCP, AWS and on-prem hybrids for regulated industries.

Anything outside those three we decline, and we point you to someone better. We take on new work only when we have the capacity to do it well.

Where is Vynatix based?

Vynatix is based in Gothenburg (Göteborg), Sweden, where Vynatix AB was founded in 2023. Its postal address is Mailbox 1611, 411 42 Gothenburg.

Does Vynatix do Android Automotive development?

Yes. Vynatix builds in-car software on Android Automotive (AAOS).

  • Many of the team come from years of automotive and embedded work.
  • For Volvo Trucks in 2024, Vynatix rebuilt the in-cab radio and media player on Android Automotive and shipped it to production across the fleet.

Can Vynatix run AI systems in our own environment, including on-prem?

Yes. Vynatix deploys AI systems into the client's own environment, including on-prem and sovereign cloud, with evaluation pipelines that show when the model is wrong and observability after it ships. If a simpler tool would serve you better than a model, we say so.

How does an engagement with Vynatix work, and how is it billed?

Vynatix places senior engineers directly inside your team and bills by the hour. An engagement runs in four steps.

  1. The first conversation is usually in person, in Gothenburg.
  2. Senior engineers join your team, with no account layer between them and you, and from then on most of the work happens in writing.
  3. The hours are reported to you every week.
  4. The engineers stay as long as the assignment needs them.

Billing works as follows.

  • You are billed for the hours worked.
  • There is no black box and no surprise invoice.
  • You own the code we write.

Who are Vynatix's clients?

Volvo Trucks and Volvo Cars are among Vynatix's clients. For Volvo Trucks, Vynatix rebuilt the in-cab radio and media player on Android Automotive; for Volvo Cars, it took a build pipeline from 47 minutes to 6 (87% shorter) on every commit. The rest of the work with both is under non-disclosure.

How is Vynatix related to Ultra Group?

Vynatix is part of Ultra Group. The company itself is Vynatix AB, org.nr 559445-6542, a software consultancy in Gothenburg, Sweden.

Our senior engineers work inside your organisation, so decisions about the work are made with the people doing it. Every project leaves a written record an engineer can read, and you own the code we write.
