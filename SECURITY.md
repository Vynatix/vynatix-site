# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **front.disk@vynatix.com** with the details. We reply to inbound within
one working day and will tell you what we can fix and when.

The same contact is published in machine-readable form at
[`/.well-known/security.txt`](.well-known/security.txt) per RFC 9116.

Please include what you found, how to reproduce it, and what you think the
impact is. If a finding needs a proof of concept, keep it to the minimum needed
to demonstrate the issue — do not access data that is not yours, degrade the
service, or run automated scanning that generates load.

We will not pursue action against anyone who reports a genuine issue in good
faith and gives us reasonable time to fix it before disclosing.

## Scope

This repository is the **public marketing website** at `vynatix.com`. It is a
static site: flat HTML, CSS and one vanilla JavaScript file, with no server-side
code, no database, no authentication, no forms and no user accounts. There is
nothing here that processes a submission.

In scope: anything served from `vynatix.com` — content injection, a broken
Content-Security-Policy, a supply-chain problem in a shipped asset, or exposure
of something that should not be published.

Out of scope: findings that require a compromised visitor device, social
engineering of our staff, denial of service through volume, missing headers that
GitHub Pages cannot send (documented under "Known and accepted" below), and
reports generated by a scanner with no demonstrated impact.

## What this site does deliberately

- **No third-party origins.** Fonts and images are served first-party. The
  Content-Security-Policy contains no external host, so a page load discloses
  nothing to anyone but our host.
- **A strict CSP**, delivered per page as a `<meta>` tag: `script-src 'self'`
  and `style-src 'self'` with no `'unsafe-inline'`, `object-src 'none'`,
  `base-uri 'self'`, `form-action 'self'`.
- **No analytics, cookies or tracking of any kind.**
- **Developer documentation is not published.** `_config.yml` keeps this file,
  `AGENTS.md`, `CLAUDE.md` and the security assessment out of the deployed site.

## Known and accepted

Some controls are HTTP response headers, and GitHub Pages cannot send them:
HSTS, `X-Content-Type-Options`, `X-Frame-Options` / `frame-ancestors`
(clickjacking), `Permissions-Policy`, and CSP violation reporting. These are
being addressed by putting a header-capable edge in front of the origin; until
that is live, please treat them as known rather than new findings.
