Services

What we do

Most of our work is building software, for the web, for desktop, for phones, for vehicles and wearables, and for the systems that sit behind all of it. Alongside that, AI is a fast-growing part of what we do, and we run cloud platforms too. If a job is not something we are genuinely good at, we will tell you so and point you to someone who is.

Application development

The software people use every day

We build the software people use every day, on whatever it needs to run on: web and desktop apps, native mobile, in-car systems, wearables, VR, and the enterprise and SaaS platforms behind them. A lot of our team comes from years in automotive and embedded work, so we are at home with the awkward, real-world constraints that come with hardware and regulated environments.

What we build
  • Web, desktop and SaaS
  • Native mobile, iOS and Android
  • Automotive and embedded systems
  • Wearables and VR
  • Enterprise platforms and integrations
AI engineering

Honest about what it can and cannot do

AI is a fast-growing part of what we do, and we build it the same way we build everything else: to run in production, and to be honest about what it can and cannot do. We can take something from a rough first prototype all the way to a tool your team uses every day, and we work in the open so you always know how it reaches its answers.

What we build
  • Chatbots and assistants
  • Retrieval and grounded answers
  • Agent and harness engineering
  • Workflow automation
  • MCP servers and integrations
  • SDKs and developer tooling
  • Evaluation, deployment and monitoring

Who we are

A small team,
on purpose.

Vynatix is a small software consultancy in Gothenburg, working since 2023 and part of Ultra Group. We keep the team small on purpose, so the people you meet are the people doing the work.

We started Vynatix because we wanted to work directly with a client's engineering team, embedded rather than kept at arm's length by an account layer. Our engineers report their hours honestly every week, and we take on new work only when we have the capacity to do it well.

We are based in Gothenburg, and most of our work is done alongside the client teams we build for. We like meeting clients face to face for that first conversation. After that, most of the work happens in writing, which keeps everyone clear on what was agreed.
