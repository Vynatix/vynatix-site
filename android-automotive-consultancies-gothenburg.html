Software consultancies in Gothenburg working on Android Automotive (2026)

Twelve software consultancies with an office in Gothenburg say publicly that they work on Android Automotive: AFRY, ALTEN, Bertrandt, GlobalLogic, Knightec Group, Knowit, Luxoft, MCA Sweden, Norvion Systems, TechLisbon, Together Tech and Vynatix, listed here alphabetically and not ranked.

Published 18 October 2026 · Checked 18 October 2026 · Compiled by Vynatix

How this list was made

The list includes consultancies that have an office in Gothenburg and publicly describe work on Android Automotive or on automotive software. Facts about the firms come from what they published themselves: their own websites, and job ads they posted on their own careers site or on Platsbanken, the Swedish Public Employment Service's job board. Facts about owners and mergers come mainly from the owners' and buyers' own pages and press releases. Each fact links to its source.

  • Tier 1. The firm says on its website that it works on Android Automotive (or AAOS), or names Android Automotive in a job ad from 2025 or 2026 for a role in Gothenburg. Job ads for other cities do not count, and neither do texts that only explain the technology.
  • Tier 2. The firm describes automotive software work in Gothenburg, such as infotainment or vehicle systems testing, but nothing we found meets the tier 1 rule.
  • Date. Every entry was checked on 18 October 2026. For job ads we also give the date the ad was published and whether it is still open.
  • Size. Headcounts appear only where the firm publishes them, and then with the firm's own figure. We do not use third-party data, such as company registers, for size.
  • No ranking. Firms are listed alphabetically within each tier. We have not assessed the quality of their work.
  • Quotes. Quotes are the firms' own words, in the original language. Swedish quotes come with our translation.

The list may be incomplete. If a firm that meets the rule is missing, or something has changed, write to front.disk@vynatix.com.

Which consultancies in Gothenburg work on Android Automotive?

Twelve consultancies with an office in Gothenburg say publicly that they work on Android Automotive, according to Vynatix's check on 18 October 2026: AFRY, ALTEN, Bertrandt, GlobalLogic, Knightec Group, Knowit, Luxoft, MCA Sweden, Norvion Systems, TechLisbon, Together Tech and Vynatix, in alphabetical order. Which is the best Android Automotive consultancy in Gothenburg?

There is no neutral ranking of Android Automotive consultancies in Gothenburg, and this list is not one: it is alphabetical and compiled by Vynatix, which is on it. Which firm fits depends on the assignment, for example how large a team you need, where in the system the work sits, and whether you want engineers inside your own team or a bounded delivery.

Compare firms on what they can show, such as projects that reached production and who actually does the work. Our Swedish guide Så väljer ni konsultbolag för mjukvaruutveckling (in Swedish) goes through the questions to ask.

How do you choose an Android Automotive consultancy?

Choose an Android Automotive consultancy by what it has delivered on the platform and by who will do the work. Ask what the firm has built on Android Automotive that reached production, and at what level of the system: apps, framework or platform-level parts such as the HAL. Ask whether you will meet the engineers who will work on the assignment, who owns the code and documentation afterwards, and whether the firm writes down what is out of scope before the work starts.

If you need a large team at short notice, a larger firm usually fits better. If you need a few senior engineers inside your own team, a smaller firm can fit.
