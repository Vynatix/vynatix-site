Questions and answers

Questions about Android Automotive work

What is Android Automotive (AAOS), and how is it different from Android Auto?

Android Automotive OS (AAOS) is a version of Android that runs natively on the car's own infotainment system, so the apps live in the vehicle itself and no phone is needed. Android Auto is different: it runs on a connected phone and projects the phone's apps onto the car's screen. Building for Android Automotive means building for the vehicle, its screens and the systems behind them, rather than for a phone.
